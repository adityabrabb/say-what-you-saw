# Background photo credits

Every photo in `public/backgrounds/` is used as a Director mode background, and each one is free to use commercially.
Photos are listed with their title, photographer, license and source page. Nothing here is used under an unclear license.

- **CC0 1.0** and **Public domain** photos need no attribution (it is given anyway, out of courtesy).
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA** photos (the first set below) require attribution, which is what this page and the in-app photo credits provide.
- All 15 "noir" photos were sourced from Wikimedia Commons file pages whose license is CC0 or public domain (many originated on Unsplash while it was CC0). They were resized to 1920x1080, slightly desaturated and darkened, and compressed as JPEG for the film's look.
- The model picks backgrounds from `backgrounds.json` using each photo's `tags`, `moods` and `time` of day.
